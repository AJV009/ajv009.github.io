## The Revolutionary Hybrid Warrior

The Oracle Red Bull Racing RB20 represents a bold evolution in Formula 1 design philosophy for the 2024 season. Described by Adrian Newey as "improved in all aspects" and by Helmut Marko as "almost a revolution," this car showcased innovative aerodynamics and advanced hybrid power technology. Car #11, piloted by Sergio Perez, competed in one of the most challenging seasons for the Mexican driver.

### Technical Innovation

The RB20 introduced groundbreaking design features:

**Hybrid Power Unit**: The Honda RBPTH002 combines a 1.6L turbocharged V6 engine with two energy recovery systems:
- MGU-K (Kinetic): 160 bhp electric motor providing ~30 seconds of power per lap
- MGU-H (Heat): Harvests energy from exhaust heat and turbocharger
- Total power output: ~1,000 horsepower
- Maximum RPM: 15,000 (regulated by F1 rules)

**Revolutionary Aerodynamics**:
- Inverted sidepod inlets inspired by Mercedes' W14 design
- Advanced cooling gulleys running from the halo along the engine cover
- Completely repositioned radiators for improved airflow
- Flattened and elongated front wing design

### Sergio Perez's 2024 Campaign

The 2024 season proved challenging for Perez in car #11:
- **Podium Finishes**: 4 (Bahrain P2, Saudi Arabia P2, Japan, China)
- **Wins**: 0 (winless campaign)
- **Pole Positions**: 0
- **Championship Points**: 152 points
- **Final Position**: 8th in Drivers' Championship

Perez started strong with back-to-back P2 finishes in Bahrain and Saudi Arabia, accumulating 103 points in the first six races. However, the season deteriorated mid-year, with frequent Q1/Q2 eliminations in qualifying. Following this difficult season, Perez and Red Bull mutually agreed to part ways, concluding his contribution to two World Constructors' Championships.

### Team Performance & Legacy

While Perez struggled, teammate Max Verstappen secured his fourth consecutive Drivers' Championship with 9 wins using the RB20. However, the team finished 3rd in the Constructors' Championship behind McLaren and Ferrari - ending their dominance streak:
- **Team Wins**: 9 (all by Verstappen)
- **Team Podiums**: 18
- **Pole Positions**: 8
- **Fastest Laps**: 4

The RB20's mid-season upgrades, particularly at the Spanish and Hungarian Grand Prix, narrowed the car's setup window and affected drivability - contributing to Red Bull's loss of the Constructors' lead to McLaren at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.
